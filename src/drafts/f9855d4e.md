Your next customer may book a heat-pump installation without loading your website once.

Picture a homeowner in Uppsala asking an AI assistant about coverage and price. Approved company data feeds the answer and booking. The homepage never enters the journey.

I think the website will remain useful for stories and proof. Its hub role is shrinking. The durable asset sits underneath the page.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>The website is becoming one possible view of company knowledge. Customers and their agents can use that knowledge without visiting the view.</p></div>

## Search became a conversation, then an action

Search keeps more answers inside its own interface, and agents can carry those answers into a task.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>AI summaries reduce the need to click. Agent protocols extend the change by letting software coordinate work after the answer.</p></div>

A [SparkToro and Datos study](https://sparktoro.com/blog/2024-zero-click-search-study/) found that 58.5% of US Google searches and 59.7% of EU searches ended without a click in 2024. The figures include abandoned and changed searches, so the number reflects a wider zero-click pattern.

[Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) measured the AI effect. People clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits with an AI summary, compared with 15% when no summary appeared. Sources inside the summary received clicks in 1% of visits.

Phil Risher attributes "2027 is the year of the agents" to Sundar Pichai, although the exact wording lacks a primary source. At Cloud Next in April 2025, Google launched the open [Agent2Agent protocol, or A2A](https://developers.googleblog.com/en/a2a-a-new-era-of-agent-interoperability/) with more than 50 technology partners. A2A lets agents coordinate work across systems.

For our heat-pump company, one agent could check coverage while another reads dispatch. ServiceTitan offers [AI virtual agents that can book field-service jobs](https://www.servicetitan.com/blog/webinar-recap-ai-virtual-agents-call-booking). I think rankings still belong beside mention rate and AI share of voice. Those measures show whether assistants include the company.

## A knowledge catalog is an approved answer set

A knowledge catalog gives the heat-pump agent one controlled place to find company answers.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>A knowledge catalog is a verified set of business facts that software can retrieve. Each answer carries enough context for safe use.</p></div>

Risher describes a knowledge catalog as a verified dataset about a business that an agent can use. The name comes from his framing. Here it means the maintained knowledge behind every digital surface.

Our example catalog could contain this entry:

```yaml
service: heat_pump_installation
region: SE-C
price_from_sek: 89000
valid_until: 2026-12-31
answer_owner: pricing@company.example
source: approved_price_list_v12
```

The values are illustrative. The shape is the point. When the agent reads `price_from_sek`, it also receives a region and expiry date. An expired entry sends the customer to a person.

Risher calls content "training data for the future internet." Runtime retrieval is safer here. The agent fetches the latest approved entry when the customer asks, so a team can correct or revoke an answer without retraining the model.

## Structured content gives the agent less room to guess

Structured content separates one business answer into fields that code can inspect.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>Typed fields make price and expiry visible to the workflow. ReAct describes the reasoning-and-action loop, while LangGraph can turn it into named steps with shared state.</p></div>

A paragraph saying "installation starts at 89,000 SEK in central Sweden" looks clear to a person. An agent still has to infer the region and validity period. The YAML entry removes that guesswork.

[ReAct](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.03629) means reasoning and acting. It is a pattern where a model picks a step, calls a tool, reads the result and continues. Our agent reads the postcode, calls the catalog and checks the entry before offering a slot.

[LangGraph](https://langchain-ai.github.io/langgraph/reference/graphs/) builds a stateful workflow as a graph. Its `StateGraph` class defines nodes and shared state. A Python `TypedDict` describes the state's keys and value types for static type checkers. Runtime safety still needs explicit validation.

```python
from typing import TypedDict
from langgraph.graph import StateGraph

class BookingState(TypedDict):
    postcode: str
    catalog_entry: dict | None
    outcome: str

workflow = StateGraph(BookingState)
workflow.add_node("lookup_catalog", lookup_catalog)
workflow.add_node("validate_entry", validate_entry)
workflow.add_node("offer_slot", offer_slot)
```

`validate_entry` can reject an expired price before `offer_slot` runs. I think structured content becomes production infrastructure when a junior engineer can point to the field and its failure path.

## A headless CMS gives you a head start

A well-modeled headless CMS has already separated approved content from the screen.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>Headless CMS platforms store reusable content and expose it through APIs. Good models can become an agent-facing catalog much faster than page copy can.</p></div>

A headless CMS stores content independently from the frontend. The website reads it through an API. An agent can read the same approved object through a controlled interface.

If the heat-pump company's prices and regions already exist as fields in Contentful, Sanity or Optimizely, the team can build a read-only catalog tool without scraping pages first. That is a structural advantage.

Someone still has to define what a standard installation includes and who updates crew capacity. My CMS view is blunt: content modeling is governance expressed as fields.

I think many teams bought headless CMS for frontend freedom and ended up preparing for agent access. That advantage depends on the model. The wider argument appears in [The quiet truth about headless CMS](/posts/the-quiet-truth-about-headless-cms/).

## The Digital Singularity moved the value below the interface

Generative UI makes the visible frontend temporary while approved knowledge keeps its value.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>GenUI can assemble a different interface for each request. Governed knowledge supplies every generated card or voice answer.</p></div>

Generative UI, usually shortened to GenUI, creates an interface for the current request. Google's [A2UI project](https://developers.googleblog.com/introducing-a2ui-an-open-project-for-agent-driven-interfaces/) gives agents a standard way to send interface descriptions to different renderers.

Our homeowner may receive a comparison card on a laptop and a voice response in the car. Both call the same catalog entry. This follows the argument in [The Digital Singularity Shift](/books/the-digital-singularity-shift/): when digital production gets cheap, value moves toward the knowledge and judgment behind the output. I think GenUI makes that movement much easier to see.

I don't know whether websites lose their hub status in three years or seven. I think the engineering response can begin now because separating durable knowledge from temporary presentation helps under either timeline.

## Choose a partner whose incentives you can inspect

The consultancy that built your content platform may already know the models and approval paths required for a first catalog.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>An existing CMS partner can move quickly when the company retains control of the catalog. Independence helps when the design crosses platforms.</p></div>

Valtech is one consultancy in this market. The partner directories for [Contentful](https://www.contentful.com/partners/), [Sanity](https://www.sanity.io/partners/) and [Optimizely](https://www.optimizely.com/partners/) offer many others.

Platform expertise can shorten the first phase. License incentives can steer the design toward another product. My take is to inspect export rights and data residency, then check revocation and payment incentives.

The incumbent partner is a sensible choice when it accepts those terms. An independent consultancy becomes more useful when the catalog spans several systems. Your organization should own the schema and test set either way.

## Build one narrow catalog now

A six-week first slice is enough to test the heat-pump booking flow.

<div class="chapter-tldr"><span class="chapter-tldr__label">In short</span><p>Start with one domain and a small set of priority questions. Ship read-only retrieval first, then add booking after the failure path works.</p></div>

Choose twenty questions about coverage and starting price. Assign a content owner and an engineering lead. Bring pricing and operations into a weekly review.

During weeks one and two, model the answers and record sources. Use weeks three and four to expose a read-only catalog tool. Spend the final two weeks testing current entries and missing postcodes. The agent should cite valid data and route uncertain cases to a person.

Measure the share of questions that return a current, approved answer. Track assistant mentions and AI share of voice as secondary signals beside rankings. [Optimizely sells automated share-of-voice monitoring](https://www.optimizely.com/agents/competitive-ai-share-of-voice-agent/), although a spreadsheet can handle the first test set.

A catalog supports compliance work through traceable ownership. Legal and risk teams also get an off switch for agent-facing claims. That beats a model scraping last year's price from an old campaign page.

Start with the answers your business is willing to stand behind when the homepage never loads.
